# The Republic / Open Cave — Charter

**Purpose.** An open-source civic AI framework that challenges institutional power by making it legible: a citizen who has hit the wall (an unreadable environmental assessment, a budget no one can parse, an FOI request they do not know how to file) gets the mechanical work of a lawyer or researcher done without giving up their own judgment.
**For.** Citizen zero is Lee, running the first real investigation on a Powell River / Eldred concern; then citizens in BC facing a government document or decision they cannot afford to understand, AB/ON next; built and run by Lee as his primary mission project, live at opencave.ca.
**Done looks like.** (1) One real investigation has run end-to-end in production: concern to briefing to Gadfly to FOI filed to outcome tracked to credential awarded. (2) Citizen data (investigations, briefings, FOI drafts, ActivityPub keys) has a real backup regime and branch protection gates main. (3) The Forum is on, credential-gated and federated. The Agora is beyond done: it stays in the phase arc, community-triggered, and steady state does not wait on it.
**Non-goals.** It does not replace the citizen's judgment; it does the mechanical comprehension work and hands back fileable documents. It does not run a social platform: credentials are earned through civic action, decay with inactivity, are soulbound, and there is no central administrator with unilateral power. It does not turn the Forum on, or certify AB/ON FOI citations, on a model's say-so; both wait on Lee and on a human practitioner respectively.
**Phase arc.** Phase 0 (Mar 2026): five arms (Oracle, Gadfly, Mirror, Lever, Scout) plus the Briefing pipeline; gate: arms compose into one civic inquiry. Governance Phases 1a-1h and 2a-2f (Apr 2026): forum, peer review, credentials, moderation, jurisdictions, federation, archive; gate: built and audited, forum feature-gated off. Production hardening (Jun-Aug 2026): audits, outage fixes, Relays 1-3, Batches B and C; gate: no irreversible harm to citizen data. (here) P2 First Light: the first real civic outcome fired in production, Lee as citizen zero; gate: the end-to-end loop actually closes. Phase 3 Agora: community-triggered (50+ active credentialed users, 3+ jurisdiction communities, credentials proven by real civic outcomes), not scheduled, beyond done.
**Drift tripwires.** Scope: has a quarter passed with shipped work that touches neither a citizen's comprehension of a government document nor a fileable civic action? Audience: is any shipped surface aimed at institutions, advocacy organizations, or pseudonymous social activity rather than a single citizen with a concern? Built-for-the-builder: has P2 First Light still never fired while design and infrastructure phases keep shipping?
Founded 2026-03-29. Rewritten only with Lee's approval.
